# Deck and pitch kit: phase 7

Build the deck with whatever slide tool is available. On claude.ai that is the Artifact tool's Slides type; otherwise use a PowerPoint or HTML deck.
- **Look:** use the venue's own palette and its traced logo. Upload the logo PNG, the best venue photo, and a desktop and a phone screenshot of each site.
- **Sources:** every number on a slide shows its source and date in a small footer.
- **Speaker notes:** say what to show live on a phone and what not to claim.

## Story
Pick the branch from `docs/web-presence.md`.
1. **Cover.** The venue's photo and logo, and a one-line hook built from its situation.
2. **What's working.** Ratings with counts, followers, a press quote. Open with respect, not criticism.
3. **Search demand.** The brand on Google Trends over time, with one competitor on the same scale.
4. **What searchers want.** Related queries (menu, photos), and who answers them today. Show where the Instagram bio and the Google listing point.
5. **The internet disagrees with itself.** The conflicts table: prices, hours, phones.
6. **Party leads on other people's forms.** Marketplace prices and capacities, and a worked example of one booking's value.
7. **What the platforms keep.** The commission and contract terms from the evidence library, on the accent-coloured slide.
8. **Branch.**
   - No site: answer "we had a website" from the archive.
   - Has a site: "your site is costing you", with its speed, weight, staleness and what it fails to answer.
9. **Competitors.** The effort audit: who manages a site that loads fast, shows the menu, lists this week's events and takes a booking.
10. **Do guests look?** Diner and Google-listing figures, labelled with their country.
11. **What the site does.**
    - One true answer.
    - A page for every night.
    - Party enquiries come straight to the venue.
    - The team updates it themselves.
    - The platforms stay.
12. **The directions.** A screenshot and the concept name for each site.
13. **Your numbers beat ours.** The owner's 90-day Google Business Profile performance and Instagram link taps, plus the discovery questions.
14. **Next steps.** Use bracketed placeholders for anything not yet decided.

## Never on a slide
- Price.
- Claims the audit does not support.
- Promises about search ranking.
- Savings figures built on rates you do not know.
- Anything marked NOT FOUND or UNVERIFIED in the evidence file.

## Pitch kit: `PITCH-KIT.md`
- **Objections and answers**, each with a sourced fact:
  - "we had a site";
  - "Instagram is enough";
  - "the apps bring us customers";
  - "too expensive";
  - "who will update it";
  - "we already have an agency".
- **Discovery questions**, and what each answer changes in scope or price.
- **Price ladder** from `docs/pricing.md`: opening, target and floor, as a one-time fee plus a monthly fee. The monthly fee pays for upkeep.
- **Pitch-day checklist:**
  - Rerun the Trends numbers.
  - Recheck the conflicts table and the events.
  - Check the domain is still free.
  - Switch the demo to its fixed URL.
  - Keep the proof pages open in browser tabs.
